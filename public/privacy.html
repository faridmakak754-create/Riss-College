Privacy Policy

We are committed to maintaining the privacy and security of student and institutional information.

This Privacy Policy outlines how our institution collects, uses, and safeguards personal data of students, staff, and visitors. We ensure transparency and accountability in handling information.

Information We Collect

The institution may collect personal details such as name, contact information, academic records, and identification details during admission and academic processes.

Use of Information

  • Academic administration and record maintenance
  • Communication with students and guardians
  • Improvement of institutional services

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. All sensitive information is handled through secure systems.

Cookies Usage

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience and improve website functionality.

User Rights

Individuals have the right to access, update, or request deletion of their personal data in accordance with institutional policies.

Our institution values trust and transparency. For any concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the administrative office.
